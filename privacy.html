My workspace

Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-21.

What this app is

My workspace is a personal publishing tool (Orbit) that runs entirely on its owner's own computer. It connects only to social media accounts that the owner controls.

Data we access

With the owner's permission, the app reads profile information, posts, comments and statistics of the owner's connected accounts, and publishes content the owner creates.

Where data is stored

All data, including access tokens, is stored locally on the owner's computer, with tokens encrypted. Nothing is stored on this website or any other server operated by the app.

Sharing

Data is never sold, rented or shared with third parties. It is only sent to the social platforms themselves, to perform actions the owner requested.

Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's settings (apps and websites). See data deletion.
