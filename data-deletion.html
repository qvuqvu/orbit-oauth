Data deletion instructions

This app stores data only on its owner's computer. To remove the app's access and data:

  1. Remove the app from your account: Facebook › Settings › Apps and websites; Instagram › Settings › Website permissions › Apps and websites; Threads › Settings › Account › Website permissions; Google › myaccount.google.com/permissions; TikTok › Settings › Security › Manage app permissions.
  2. In Orbit, open Channels and remove the account — its tokens and synced data are deleted from the computer immediately.

For any request, contact qvuqvuqvuqvu@gmail.com. Requests are completed within 7 days.
